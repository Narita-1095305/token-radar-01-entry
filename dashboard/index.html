HOW THE SIGNAL IS BUILT

Comparable markets. Explicit assumptions.

01 / Establish a baseline

For snapshot protocols with market cap and positive TVL, divide market cap by TVL and clamp each ratio to 0.01×–5×. Use the category median with four or more observations. For fewer, blend it with the global median using count ÷ 4 as category weight.

02 / Adjust the signal

TVL × category multiple × growth × age × chains. Growth is 1 + 1.5 × (7d change ÷ 100), capped at 0.70–1.60. Age follows 0.75 + 0.5 × years ÷ (years + 1.5); unknown age uses 0.80. Each additional chain adds 3%, up to five.

03 / Read with care

Estimates are rounded and clamped to $100K–$100B. All watchlist rows are retained; eligible additional rows fill toward 240, or fewer when the eligible set is smaller. “Records no token” describes the snapshot’s symbol and CoinGecko fields, not proof of token absence. Plans are exploratory, with estimated costs; rewards are not guaranteed.

Additional rows exclude the specified bridge, staking and exchange categories, wrapped or staked names, version suffixes and exchange prefixes. Comparable ratios are descriptive; they do not establish a future token price or entitlement.
